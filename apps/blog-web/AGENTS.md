# blog-web

Pure Vue SPA migration, parallel to `apps/blog`. Follow apps/blog's Home: the card Home and hypercube scene (the Home film was retired in 06a399e), the author panel at `/author`, `/author/1-4` and `/1-4`, route names, aliases and the single mounted HomeView. Mobile adaptation is deferred by the user.

Use pnpm from the monorepo root. Generated content is never manually edited or committed. During migration static assets still originate in the legacy content pipeline; do not delete the legacy app or content source before the plan's cutover gates.
